# Carmack recordings and Romero 2005

Load Romero_Carmack_Duo_Voices_2005.pk3 alone instead of the older companion package.

All five supplied Carmack interview clips are included in full, converted to mono Ogg Vorbis for the game. They last 44-69 seconds. After the initial 60-second grace period, idle/kill chatter can trigger a clip. The default pause is 180 seconds after a clip ends, with no immediate repeat. Other companion speech waits while a clip plays. Interviews are not used as death reactions. Disabling Carmack interview voice stops the current recording.

Options > Friend Options contains Carmack interview voice and Carmack pause after clip (seconds). Romero tips remain disabled for solo Carmack.

John Romero 2005 is available under both Solo companion skin and Romero skin when together. It has long black hair, a light striped shirt and pendant, eight directions, and a bloodless collapse/revival. The independent Romero HUD portrait selector is unchanged.
