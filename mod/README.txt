John Romero - Daikatana 1200 BC Greece Companion

Combined build based on FriendJohn and DoomKatana Weapons: 1200 BC Greece.

John uses companion-only versions of the Glove, Hammer of Hades,
Discus of Daedalus, Staff of Venom, Trident of Poseidon, Sunflare,
and Eye of Zeus attacks, with the original Daikatana sounds and effects.
Player weapons, pickups, player classes, and gameplay HUD are not replaced.

The top-right portrait glances left and right at random intervals and shows
John's kill count for the current map, resetting on each new level. Choose Glasses John or Classic John under:
Options > Friend Options > John HUD portrait.

The same menu lets John switch between balanced Daikatana and classic Doom
weapon sets. At the end of each level, the Doom tally screen includes John's
animated portrait, his kills from that level, and a performance-based remark.
The tally addition can be disabled under Options > Friend Options.

At the start of every fresh map, John also shows one randomly selected fact
from a pool of 100 facts about Doom, Quake, and Daikatana. The immediately
previous fact is not repeated. This can be switched off under:
Options > Friend Options > John game facts.

The supplied John Romero Companion artwork is used as the Escape main-menu
background.
