# Original fictional companion dialogue

All 250 lines in NNR_Banter.zsc are original game dialogue, not quotations or endorsements. Research informed broad character themes only.

- Romero: enthusiasm for playing, level design, space and movement. Primary interview: https://tim.blog/2023/07/15/john-romero-transcript/
- Carmack: analytical engineering, performance, resource tradeoffs. Primary interview: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/carmack-on-i-rage-i-
- Wilbur: practical business support, distribution, getting games to players. Primary interview: https://entrepreneursmind.com/id-software/

Examples of invented lines: Romero, Cyberdemon: "Cyberdemon down! Now THAT was a fight." Carmack, imp: "Imp down. The projectile was predictable." Jay, BFG: "BFG. Budget approved."

- Tom Hall: playful science fiction, character/story design, puns and self-made sound effects. Primary interview: https://episodiccontentmag.com/2015/04/17/tomhall2/
- Adrian Carmack: monster art, silhouettes and economical animation. Interview excerpts reported here: https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/early-90s-animation-tools-were-so-primitive-that-dooms-lead-artist-says-he-pretty-much-did-the-whole-thing-with-the-undo-key/

Tom and Adrian dialogue is a fictional creative interpretation of these themes, not a claim about their actual speech. Each has 50 lines across the same 25 situations; both skins share dialogue.
