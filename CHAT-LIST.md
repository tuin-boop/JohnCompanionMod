# Current companion chat list

These are original fictional game lines, not authentic quotations. Extracted from NNR_Banter.zsc.

Romero, John Carmack and Jay each have 50 lines. Tom Hall and Adrian currently have no text banter or borrowed voice recordings. Skins share the character’s dialogue.

## When text appears

- Requires Chat ON. No chat on the title map; hidden while a menu is open.
- Ordinary kills: 12% chance to request a line. Cyberdemon, Spider Mastermind and other bosses: 85%. Cooldowns can still reject a request.
- For non-boss kill requests, 40% attempt a weapon-specific line; otherwise enemy-specific or generic. Weapon lines occur on kills, not every shot.
- Shared cooldown: 30–50 seconds. Each speaker also waits at least 75 seconds between their own lines.
- Voice audio already playing blocks new text lines. Text remains for 7 seconds and fades during its last second.
- First quiet introduction: eligible after 10 seconds into the map. If unavailable, retried every 15 seconds. The introduction flag is per player, not per companion.
- After a successful line, idle/camp chat waits 90–150 seconds. It requires a companion within 2048 map units, no valid combat target, and no shots in the last 5 seconds. If unmet, retried every 15 seconds.
- A selected eligible companion is picked randomly for quiet chat. Camping changes which pair of quiet lines is used.
- Each category has two variants; the last variant is avoided on the next use of that category by that speaker.

## Recorded audio (separate from text chat)

- Romero retains the bundled voice triggers. Idle timing uses the Romero Idle Chat setting; 500 tics is about 14.3 seconds.
- John Carmack has five supplied interview clips. Carmack Interviews toggles these; Carmack Pause controls the delay after a clip (default 180 seconds, minimum 60). A clip must finish before the next pause ends.
- Jay, Tom and Adrian do not play borrowed Romero or John Carmack recordings.
